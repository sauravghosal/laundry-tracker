Linen Inventory vs. Par

In Cabins (1×) is fixed at each item's 1× property total, since every cabin is assumed clean & stocked — it's counted toward the total automatically and isn't editable. Packed and Dirty Returns are set by the cabin pickers above — check/uncheck cabins to change them. Clean Shelf, Secret Stash, and Unopened are entered by hand. Dirty Sorted is dirty linen already sorted and coming back from commercial — bag counts aren't exact, so enter your best estimate per item. Par (2× the property totals) still drives Status behind the scenes but isn't shown as its own column. Order (dz) starts at 0 — enter how many dozens you're ordering. New Par is (Total + Order) ÷ 1× par (the property total) — how many pars you'd be carrying if that order came in; 2.00× means you'd be fully stocked to the 2× target.
